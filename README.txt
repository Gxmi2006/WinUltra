WinUltra
========
A glass Dynamic Island, a desktop clock and a floating taskbar for Windows 11, in one small app.

Installing
----------
1. Double-click WinUltra.exe. It installs itself for your account (no admin needed),
   starts, and will start with Windows from now on.
2. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC", because the app isn't from a known
   publisher. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Using it
--------
- Island: hover the glowing line at the top of the screen, or press Ctrl+Alt+Space.
  Rest the mouse on a tab (Media, Controls, Stats, Clipboard, Shelf, Timer) to open it.
- Clipboard picker: Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V in any app, then click an item to paste it.
- Shelf: drop files on the island. Pin files, folders or apps to keep them as launchers.
- Clock: right-click it on the desktop and choose Edit mode to move or resize it.
- Tray icon (bottom right, maybe under the ^ arrow): turn the clock or the floating
  taskbar on or off, stop starting with Windows, quit, or uninstall.

It needs Windows 11 (64-bit). Nothing is sent anywhere; settings, clipboard pins and the
shelf stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinUltra on your PC.
